## Описание

Nodes (groups and leaves) with world = parentWorld * local, 2x3 affine per layer sent to the
renderer (supports shear for children of rotated/non-uniformly scaled groups), tap selects the
top-level group and further taps drill down, group bounding frame in screen space, drag deltas
converted to parent coordinates. Clipped layers without a leaf base are drawn normally.
